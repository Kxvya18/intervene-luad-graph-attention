# Biology Evidence Summary for Deep-Biology Review

## Final biological claim
High-risk NSCLC tumors show a candidate adaptive-stress relapse program involving proteostasis burden, redox buffering, AKT/mTOR survival signaling, mitochondrial translation, apoptosis/IAP escape, and SREBP/lipid remodeling.

## Not just proliferation
In GSE31210, GraphSparse V3 remains predictive after adjustment for proliferation/cell-cycle expression, and after clinical + proliferation adjustment.

## Independent external validation
GSE50081 provides an independent early-stage NSCLC cohort with disease-free survival time and recurrence labels. The fixed, training-free INTERVENE state is tested against recurrence/DFS.

## Clinical independence
The current GSE31210 V3 score remains significant beyond stage, age, smoking, driver status, and proliferation.

## Drug/vulnerability bridge
The refined PRISM bridge separates proteostasis/epigenetic stress, apoptosis/IAP, mitochondrial/OXPHOS, redox, AKT/mTOR, generic cytotoxic, and direct lipid classes. Direct lipid remains a weak/secondary result; the stronger hypothesis is stress-buffering network vulnerability.

## Careful wording
This is not proof of relapse causation or therapy response. It is a computationally validated, biologically grounded hypothesis that relapse-prone NSCLC tumors may depend on a coordinated stress-buffering network.
