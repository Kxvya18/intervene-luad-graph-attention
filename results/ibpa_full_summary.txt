Full Interleaved Biological-Physics Attention prototype
======================================================

Implementation note:
This file now contains two IBPA modes. IBPA-v2 is the main discovery mode: it learns Q/K/V projections, alpha_Q/alpha_K/alpha_V interleaved pseudo-head mixing, and module attention by reconstructing paper-facing biological-physics module states with a weak thermodynamic/SREBP/mitochondrial physics prior. PRISM is then used only for validation/enrichment. The supervised PRISM-prediction models are kept as audit comparisons.
