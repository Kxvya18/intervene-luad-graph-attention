Careful interpretation
----------------------
GraphSparse V3 should be described as a relapse-associated stress-survival hypothesis generator. The gene, driver, and therapy tables are not proof of treatment response; they define testable hypotheses connecting sparse module edges to NSCLC relapse risk.
