Stage 1 Interleaved Biological-Physics Attention prototype
=========================================================

Method note:
This is a small interpretable IHA-inspired prototype, not a full transformer. Biological/physics heads are z-scored per NSCLC cell line. Interleaved pseudo-heads are pairwise head products between non-target biological/physics heads. The prediction target is PRISM stress-survival sensitivity, with a specific target also reported after penalizing generic cytotoxic sensitivity.
