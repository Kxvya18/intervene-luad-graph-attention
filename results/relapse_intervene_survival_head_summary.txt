Interpretation
--------------
A good Relapse-INTERVENE result is not only higher C-index. It should also preserve the validated biology: high-risk patients should be enriched for relapse, and the interleaved attention edges should repeatedly connect lipid/SREBP, mitochondrial translation, oxidative stress, proteostasis, apoptosis/IAP, and AKT/mTOR modules.
